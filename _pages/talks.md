  <h2 class="section__title">Telescope programs</h2>

  <p class="measure text-body">
    Programs I lead as Principal Investigator, plus the facilities where I contribute as a
    co-investigator through the DLT40, SHADOW, PASSTA, AZTEC, SAGUARO, GSP and REFITT
    collaborations.
  </p>

  <div class="grid grid--2 mt-2">
    <div class="card">
      <span class="card__meta">Space &middot; PI</span>
      <h3 class="card__title">JWST &amp; HST</h3>
      <p>
        <strong>JWST Cycle&nbsp;5 GO-11424</strong> (PI, 7.6&nbsp;h): resolving the
        host galaxy of AT&nbsp;2021lwx and testing SMBH&ndash;host scaling relations for
        extreme nuclear transients.<br><br>
        <strong>HST Cycle&nbsp;32 GO-17748</strong> (PI, 3 orbits): observations of
        AT&nbsp;2021lwx.<br><br>
        Co-investigator on JWST programs watching dust form in real time in two very nearby
        core-collapse supernovae (GO-7881, GO-12572) and on NIR+MIR spectroscopy of the
        broad-lined Type&nbsp;Ic SN&nbsp;2024abup (DD-06803). 20.8&nbsp;h of JWST time
        across three programs.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <span class="card__meta">Ground &middot; PI</span>
      <h3 class="card__title">Keck, Gemini, Magellan, MMT, Bok</h3>
      <p>
        <strong>Keck</strong> and <strong>Gemini</strong> (PI): searching for
        late-time circumstellar-interaction and dust-formation signatures in normal
        core-collapse supernovae.<br><br>
        <strong>Magellan</strong> and <strong>MMT</strong> (PI, 2026B and 2025B):
        follow-up of electromagnetic counterparts to gravitational-wave events in O4 and
        O4-IR1.<br><br>
        <strong>Bok/90Prime</strong> (PI, 15 nights): a ZTF pilot for early transient
        science ahead of LSST, and pre-surveys laying groundwork for ULTRASAT.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <span class="card__meta">Surveys &middot; Co-I</span>
      <h3 class="card__title">DECam, SOAR &amp; LCO</h3>
      <p>
        <strong>SHADOW</strong> on CTIO/DECam (77.1 nights, 2025B&ndash;2028A):
        shadowing LSST to find the youngest transients in the nearby universe.<br><br>
        <strong>PASSTA</strong> on SOAR (60 nights): the public AEON spectroscopic
        survey for transient astronomy.<br><br>
        More than 100 nights on <strong>Las Cumbres Observatory</strong> through the Global
        Supernova Project and REFITT programs.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
